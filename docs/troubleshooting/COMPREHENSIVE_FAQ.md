## General Questions

### What is Blytz Live Auction?

Blytz Live Auction is a real-time online auction platform that combines live video streaming with interactive bidding. Users can participate in live auctions, watch product demonstrations, and bid on items in real-time.

### How does Blytz work?

1. **Browse Products**: Explore available items and upcoming auctions
2. **Join Live Auctions**: Watch live video demonstrations and interact with sellers
3. **Place Bids**: Bid in real-time against other participants
4. **Win and Pay**: Successful bidders complete payment through secure checkout
5. **Receive Items**: Sellers ship products to winning bidders

### What makes Blytz different from other auction sites?

- **Live Video Streaming**: Watch real-time product demonstrations
- **Interactive Chat**: Communicate directly with sellers and other bidders
- **Real-time Bidding**: See bids placed instantly
- **Mobile-First Design**: Optimized for mobile devices
- **Secure Payments**: Multiple payment options with buyer protection

### Is Blytz available in my country?

Blytz is currently available in:
- United States
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- Australia
- European Union

We're expanding to additional countries. Check our website for the latest availability.

### What are the system requirements?

**Desktop/Laptop:**
- Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
- Stable internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps)
- Webcam and microphone (for hosting auctions)

**Mobile:**
- iOS 14+ or Android 8+
- Latest version of Blytz app
- Stable internet connection (minimum 3 Mbps)

## Account and Authentication

### How do I create an account?

1. Click "Sign Up" on the website or in the app
2. Enter your email address and create a password
3. Fill in your profile information
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete your profile to unlock all features

### What are the password requirements?

Passwords must:
- Be at least 8 characters long
- Contain at least one uppercase letter
- Contain at least one lowercase letter
- Contain at least one number
- Contain at least one special character (!@#$%^&*)

### How do I reset my password?

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Click the reset link (valid for 24 hours)
5. Create a new password
6. Log in with your new password

### How do I enable two-factor authentication?

1. Go to Account Settings > Security
2. Click "Enable Two-Factor Authentication"
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app
4. Enter the verification code to confirm
5. Save backup codes in a secure location

### Why was my account suspended?

Accounts may be suspended for:
- Violation of terms of service
- Suspicious activity detected
- Multiple payment chargebacks
- Fraudulent bidding behavior
- Incomplete verification requirements

Contact support to appeal account suspension.

### How do I delete my account?

1. Go to Account Settings > Privacy
2. Click "Delete Account"
3. Read the consequences and confirm
4. Enter your password to verify
5. Your account will be deleted within 30 days

**Note**: Deleted accounts cannot be recovered. Download your data before deletion.

## Auctions and Bidding

### How do I find auctions to bid on?

1. **Browse Categories**: Navigate through product categories
2. **Search**: Use keywords to find specific items
3. **Filter Results**: Filter by price, condition, location
4. **Watchlist**: Save interesting auctions for later
5. **Notifications**: Set alerts for specific criteria

### How does bidding work?

1. **Minimum Bid**: Each auction has a minimum bid amount
2. **Bid Increments**: Bids must increase by set increments
3. **Real-time Updates**: See current bid and time remaining
4. **Auto-bid**: Set maximum bid and system bids for you
5. **Anti-snipe**: Auction extends if bids placed in final minutes

### What is anti-snipe protection?

Anti-snipe protection extends auction time if bids are placed in the final minutes:
- Any bid in last 2 minutes extends auction by 5 minutes
- Maximum extension: 30 minutes total
- Ensures all bidders have fair chance to respond

### How do I place a bid?

1. **Find Auction**: Browse to the auction you want
2. **Enter Amount**: Type your bid amount
3. **Confirm Bid**: Review and confirm your bid
4. **Receive Confirmation**: Get instant bid confirmation
5. **Monitor Auction**: Watch for competing bids

### What happens if I win an auction?

1. **Win Notification**: Receive immediate win notification
2. **Payment Window**: 48 hours to complete payment
3. **Checkout Process**: Enter shipping and payment details
4. **Order Confirmation**: Receive order details and tracking
5. **Seller Notification**: Seller ships item to you

### Can I cancel my bid?

**Before Auction Ends**:
- Bids cannot be cancelled once placed
- Ensure you're willing to pay before bidding

**After Auction Ends**:
- If you win, you must complete payment
- Contact support immediately if you made an error

### What are bid increments?

Bid increments are set by sellers and vary by auction:

| Current Bid Range | Minimum Increment |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| $0.01 - $25 | $1.00 |
| $25.01 - $100 | $2.50 |
| $100.01 - $250 | $5.00 |
| $250.01 - $500 | $10.00 |
| $500.01 - $1,000 | $25.00 |
| $1,000.01 - $2,500 | $50.00 |
| $2,500.01+ | $100.00 |

### How do I become a seller?

1. **Create Account**: Sign up and verify your email
2. **Complete Profile**: Add seller information and verification
3. **Verify Identity**: Complete identity verification process
4. **Connect Payment**: Set up payment receiving methods
5. **List First Auction**: Create your first auction listing

### What are the seller fees?

During beta testing, all fees are waived. After public launch:
- **Listing Fee**: $0.99 per auction
- **Success Fee**: 5% of final sale price
- **Payment Processing**: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction

### How do I host a live auction?

1. **Schedule Auction**: Create auction with live streaming enabled
2. **Test Equipment**: Verify camera and microphone work
3. **Start Live Stream**: Click "Go Live" at scheduled time
4. **Demonstrate Product**: Show item from multiple angles
5. **Manage Bids**: Monitor and accept bids in real-time

## Payments and Refunds

### What payment methods are accepted?

- **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Digital Wallets**: Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal
- **Bank Transfer**: For high-value items (over $1,000)

### Is my payment information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard security:
- **SSL Encryption**: All data encrypted in transit
- **PCI Compliance**: Payment industry standards
- **Tokenization**: Card details stored securely
- **Fraud Detection**: Advanced fraud prevention systems

### How do I pay for an auction I won?

1. **Win Notification**: Receive email and in-app notification
2. **Go to Checkout**: Click "Pay Now" from notification
3. **Enter Details**: Add shipping and payment information
4. **Review Order**: Confirm all details are correct
5. **Complete Payment**: Submit payment and receive confirmation

### What is the payment deadline?

You have **48 hours** to complete payment after winning an auction:
- Payment reminder sent at 24 hours
- Final reminder sent at 36 hours
- Auction cancellation if not paid by 48 hours

### How do refunds work?

**Buyer Protection**:
- Request refund within 14 days of receipt
- Item not as described or damaged
- Seller didn't ship item

**Refund Process**:
1. Contact seller first to resolve issue
2. If unresolved, request refund through platform
3. Provide evidence and documentation
4. Platform mediates and processes refund

**Refund Timeline**:
- Digital goods: 3-5 business days
- Physical goods: 7-10 business days after return

### Why was my payment declined?

Common reasons:
- **Insufficient Funds**: Check account balance
- **Incorrect Information**: Verify card details and billing address
- **Security Alert**: Bank flagged suspicious activity
- **Expired Card**: Check card expiration date
- **Daily Limit**: Reached daily spending limit

**Solutions**:
- Try different payment method
- Contact your bank
- Update payment information
- Try again later

## Live Streaming

### What do I need for live streaming?

**Minimum Requirements**:
- Internet connection: 5 Mbps upload speed
- Camera: 720p HD webcam or better
- Microphone: Built-in or external microphone
- Browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)

**Recommended Setup**:
- Internet: 10+ Mbps upload speed
- Camera: 1080p HD webcam
- Lighting: Good front lighting
- Audio: External microphone for better sound quality

### How do I improve video quality?

1. **Internet Connection**: Use wired connection if possible
2. **Lighting**: Ensure good front lighting
3. **Background**: Use clean, non-distracting background
4. **Camera Position**: Place camera at eye level
5. **Audio Quality**: Use external microphone in quiet room

### Can I record my live streams?

Yes, sellers can:
- **Auto-Record**: Streams automatically recorded
- **Download Recordings**: Save for future use
- **Share Recordings**: Use for marketing or training
- **Privacy Settings**: Control who can view recordings

### What if my stream disconnects?

**Immediate Steps**:
1. **Check Internet**: Verify connection is stable
2. **Refresh Browser**: Close and reopen browser
3. **Restart Stream**: Click "Go Live" again
4. **Contact Support**: Report persistent issues

**Prevention**:
- Test equipment before going live
- Use wired internet connection
- Close other bandwidth-heavy applications
- Have backup internet connection ready

1. Restart the app

**iOS**:
1. Go to Settings > Notifications > Blytz
2. Enable "Allow Notifications"
3. Choose notification types (bids, wins, messages)

**Android**:
1. Go to Settings > Apps > Blytz > Notifications
2. Enable all notification categories
3. Check "Do Not Disturb" settings

### Can I use the mobile app and website simultaneously?

Yes, you can:
- **Synced Experience**: Actions sync across devices
- **Real-time Updates**: See bids and messages instantly
- **Seamless Switching**: Start on mobile, continue on desktop
- **Single Account**: Same account works on all platforms

## Developer Questions

### How do I access the API?

**API Documentation**: [docs/api/API_DOCUMENTATION_INDEX.md](../api/API_DOCUMENTATION_INDEX.md)

**Authentication**:
- Register for API key in developer portal
- Use JWT tokens for authenticated requests
- Follow rate limiting guidelines

**Base URLs**:
- Development: `http://localhost:8092/api/v1`
- Staging: `https://staging.blytz.app/api/v1`
- Production: `https://api.blytz.app/api/v1`

### Are there SDKs available?

**Official SDKs**:
- **JavaScript/TypeScript**: `npm install @blytz/api-client`
- **Go**: `go get github.com/gmsas95/blytz-live-sdk-go`
- **Python**: `pip install blytz-api-client`

**Community SDKs**:
- Check GitHub for community-maintained SDKs
- Verify compatibility with your use case

### How do I report API issues?

**API Issue Reporting**:
1. **Endpoint**: Which API endpoint failed
2. **Request**: Full request details (headers, body)
3. **Response**: Error response received
4. **Timestamp**: When the issue occurred
5. **Reproduction**: Steps to reproduce the issue

**Report Format**:
```json
{
  "api_endpoint": "/api/v1/auctions",
  "request_method": "POST",
  "request_body": {...},
  "error_response": {...},
  "timestamp": "2025-12-11T07:00:00Z",
  "reproduction_steps": ["1. Login", "2. Go to auction", "3. Place bid"]
}
```

### What are the API rate limits?

**Rate Limits**:
- **Authentication**: 10 requests per minute
- **Standard Endpoints**: 100 requests per minute
- **Payment Endpoints**: 20 requests per minute

**Rate Limit Headers**:
```http
X-RateLimit-Limit: 100
X-RateLimit-Remaining: 95
X-RateLimit-Reset: 1640995200
```
