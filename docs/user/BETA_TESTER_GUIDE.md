## Bidding Guide

### How to Place a Bid

1. **Find an Auction**: Browse or search for items
2. **Review Details**: Check item description, condition, and seller info
3. **Place Bid**:
   - Enter your maximum bid amount
   - Review bid increment rules
   - Confirm your bid
4. **Confirmation**: Receive bid confirmation via email/app notification

### Bidding Strategies

#### 1. Maximum Bidding
- Set your highest willing price
- System automatically bids incrementally
- Avoids last-minute bidding wars

#### 2. Last-Minute Bidding
- Place bids close to auction end
- Watch for anti-snipe protection
- Risk of missing the deadline

#### 3. Proxy Bidding
- Let the system bid for you
- Set maximum comfortable price
- Monitor auction activity

### Bid Increments

| Current Bid | Minimum Increment |
|-------------|------------------|
| $0.01 - $25 | $1.00 |
| $25.01 - $100 | $2.50 |
| $100.01 - $250 | $5.00 |
| $250.01 - $500 | $10.00 |
| $500.01 - $1,000 | $25.00 |
| $1,000.01 - $2,500 | $50.00 |
| $2,500.01+ | $100.00 |

### Anti-Snipe Protection

- **Extension**: Auction extends by 5 minutes if bid placed in last 2 minutes
- **Fairness**: Ensures all bidders have equal opportunity
- **Limit**: Maximum 30-minute extension per auction

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: How do I become a verified seller?**
A: Complete your profile, verify your email and phone, then submit identity verification documents.

**Q: What are the fees for using Blytz?**
A: During beta testing, all fees are waived. Final fee structure will be announced before public launch.

**Q: Can I cancel a bid?**
A: Bids cannot be cancelled once placed. Please bid carefully.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why is the video quality poor?**
A: Check your internet connection speed and close other applications using bandwidth.

**Q: The app crashed during bidding. What happens to my bid?**
A: Your bid is still valid if it was successfully submitted. Check your email for confirmation.

**Q: I can't hear the live stream audio.**
A: Check your device volume settings and ensure you've granted microphone permissions.

### Payment Questions

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and are PCI compliant.

**Q: What if the seller doesn't ship the item?**
A: Contact support within 7 days of payment confirmation for resolution.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds are handled according to the seller's return policy. Check the auction listing before bidding.
